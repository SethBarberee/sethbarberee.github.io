Huh, would you look at that? Another post has finally dropped. I'm typing this
on the Amtrak Empire Builder somewhere in Minnesota. I am on a nice two week
rail vacation where I took the Acela, Cardinal, and the Empire Builder. I will
still take the Coast Starlight in a few days from Seattle to get back to San
Jose. In the meantime, let's catch up, shall we?

## Homelab

So, uh, the homelab is turned off. California + High Energy bills = Not good
for Seth's wallet. It was a fun learning experience that I might push to
later. The only thing that I might do in the future is build a NAS to store
all my media and stream my anime from that. I experimented with Jellyfin and
used a USB HDD attached to my ASUS Router and was amazed at the success.
Albeit, the very hot HDD probably wasn't happy and 4TB isn't enough for all
the anime that I would like to watch.

## Train Reviews

* Acela - 9/10: OMG, I love how fast I'm flying and Wi-Fi is pretty solid. We
  need more trains like this in the US. 8 hours from Boston to DC is insane.
* Cardinal - 7/10: Not too bad, to be honest. 24 hours but only has cafe car.
  The food is fine for shorter trips but you kind of start to hate the cafe
after 12 hours. The views are pretty solid such as the New River Gorge in West
Virginia
* Empire Builder - 7/10: Pretty good. Mainly, I got tired of the train after
two days in my seat. Don't get me wrong that the sights were great and I had a
nice time to myself on it. Weaving from flat Montana to gorgeous Washington,
lots of pretty sights.
* Coast Starlight - 8/10: Just like I remembered but going south this time
back home from Seattle to San Jose. Just a single day aboard and we were on
time to San Jose, so I was pretty pleased.
